## Fit with Match Writing Voice

The personal profile remains above generic editorial advice. Repetition, long coordinated sentences, ordinary words such as **Overall** and **important**, and supported imperfections can remain. A voice transfer is not a cleanup-only request; editing depth follows the actual action requested. Explicit user preferences continue to outrank observed habits within their scopes.

Three content judgments stay separate: what the writer likely knows, what the reader needs explained, and where a claim came from. Shared reader context cannot establish writer expertise, and newly retrieved material cannot create a claim that the writer just learned it. Source details remain accounted for during a complete rewrite even when they would be unnecessary in a short reply.

The substance review is purpose-aware. A greeting or feeling does not need a number, a notification does not always need an ask, and a deliberate recap can add emphasis without adding a new fact. A missing required fact is a gap to identify, not a license to delete the claim or invent support.

The Unicode inspector reports selected codepoints, names, counts, and positions in supplied UTF-8 text. It never rewrites the source or decides a character's linguistic role. Joiners, direction controls, selectors, and spaces can be meaningful. This is text-layer inspection, not an AI detector, normalizer, metadata scrubber, or watermark test.

## Validation boundary

Repository tests exercise source preservation, input/output alias protection, multilingual/display characters, offsets, counts, package contents, notice inclusion, and reproducible builds. The existing evidence, editing-mode, rewrite, and knowledge checks remain applicable. Independent synthetic model exercises can test decisions under the instructions, but cannot establish real author recognition or guarantee unchanged outputs across model runs. Actual voice recognition remains an author evaluation.
